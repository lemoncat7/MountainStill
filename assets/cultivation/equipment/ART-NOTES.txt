TRUE PAPER-DOLL EQUIPMENT, original AI-generated watercolor-style artwork

This folder contains a neutral adult male cultivator base and eight separate
transparent equipment overlays. Base always wears opaque loose white shorts.
No equipment file includes a face, body, hand, or unrelated garment slot.

All runtime layers share a 512 x 512 transparent canvas with zero offset and
unit scale. Draw every selected layer at exactly the same destination rectangle.
Foot anchor: (256, 496). Right-hand weapon grip anchor: (318, 414).
Suggested order: base, legs, footwear, chest, head accessory, weapon.
Independent slot selection is supported, including an empty slot. The base is
always present, so clearing leg equipment reveals modest shorts.

Reproducible pipeline
Original generated sources are retained in sources/. tools/crop_equipment_assets.py
only crops, scales and places the source garment artwork onto matching canvases.
It does not recolor or draw costumes over full character sprites. Exact target
rectangles and runtime anchors are in equipment-anchors.json.

Generation method
Built-in image generation, with the supplied Chinese storybook image as style
reference. Base prompt: original youthful adult male cultivator; neutral,
front-facing relaxed stance; chibi oversized head and compact body; jade-blue
swept hair and small topknot; blue-green eyes; delicate ink, soft cel shading and
watercolor texture; shirtless with understated anatomy; always opaque loose
white knee-length shorts; bare feet; no jewelry, props, background or shadow.
The first base had too realistic proportions, so an image-generation edit made
the head/body proportions more strongly chibi while retaining the adult facial
features, modest shorts, relaxed pose and clean alpha.

Equipment generation prompt: isolated true-alpha 2-column x 4-row paper-doll
atlas, only eight garments in the base's front-facing pose; no skin, face, hair,
hands, body or bare feet. Row 1: cloud hairpin with blue jade ribbon; small gold
and jade cloud crown. Row 2: blue short wrap robe tunic with white lapels, cloud
embroidery, short sleeves and waist sash; sage closed vest with ivory/gold short
shoulder cape. Both end at waist and have transparent neck openings. Row 3:
blue short loose trousers with overlapping pale-blue robe skirt panels; sage
cropped loose trousers with ivory belt and gold cuffs. Both fully cover base
shorts. Row 4: matching pair of ivory/blue cloth shoes; matching pair of sage and
gold boots. Clothes render in worn front-facing shape, not flat-lay. Keep every
slot separate and independently combinable; no label, grid, shadow or background.
