# Raw evidence location

Public source packaging intentionally excludes `.log` files; that guard was not relaxed and files were not renamed to bypass it. Full original failures, verbose logs, native screenshots and reproduction evidence remain in the companion MountainStill-v1.3.12-integrated-QA.tar.gz Library artifact. The source retains summaries, numeric JSON observations, hashes, and test/runner source. References to `.log` files in historical reports refer to that companion archive.
